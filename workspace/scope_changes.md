# SoopiFood Release 1 — scope changes

Capacity remains 50 points.

## Decisions

| Change request | Points | Decision | Trade-off | Why |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| CR-01 — Show allergen information on every dish | 2 | Must | Demote SF-09 — Reorder a previous order in one tap from Could to Won't. | The regulation makes allergen information mandatory at launch. |
| CR-02 — Split the bill between friends in a group order | 8 | Won't | None, because it adds no planned effort. | Marketing says the campaign can proceed without it. |

Assumption: SF-09 — Reorder a previous order in one tap is selected because it is a two-point Could item and exactly funds the mandatory change.
