|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NFR-01 | Performance | New-order notifications shall reach the tablet within **[TBC] seconds** of order submission. Question: What notification delay is acceptable? | Dinner peak, 18:00–21:00 | Load test and measure submission-to-notification latency at the agreed percentile. | FR-01 |
| NFR-02 | Performance | A tablet action shall receive local confirmation within **[TBC] milliseconds**. | NFR-03 | Reliability | The tablet shall retain at least **[TBC]%** of confirmed local changes during a connectivity loss of up to **[TBC] minutes**. | NFR-04 | Reliability | The tablet shall synchronize at least **[TBC]%** of queued changes within **[TBC] seconds** after connectivity returns, with no more than **[TBC]** duplicates. | NFR-05 | Security | The tablet and order service shall store **0** primary account numbers or card verification values. | All environments | Inspect schemas, logs, storage, and representative transactions for prohibited card data. | FR-24 |

| NFR-07 | Scalability | The order service shall meet NFR-01 and NFR-02 while processing **[TBC] orders per minute** across **[TBC] restaurants**. | NFR-08 | Usability | At least **[TBC]%** of representative managers shall identify a new order and its promised time within **[TBC] seconds** from **[TBC] metres** away. | NFR-09 | Maintainability | Automated tests shall cover at least **[TBC]%** of changed executable statements before release. Question: What coverage gate does the team adopt? | Each pull request | Enforce a changed-code coverage gate in CI. | FR-01–FR-24 |
| NFR-10 | Portability | The tablet application shall complete at least **[TBC]%** of critical workflows on every supported OS version and screen size listed in the **[TBC] support matrix**. | NFR-11 | Compliance | Customer contact information shall be removed from restaurant access within **[TBC] minutes** after order closure.
