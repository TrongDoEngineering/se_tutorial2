# Interview guide — round 1

## Warm-up

1. Could you describe a typical shift and how you juggle your responsibilities?
   - What usually needs your attention during the dinner peak?
   - Where do delivery orders fit into the shift?

## Current workflow

2. Walk me through a delivery order from arrival to rider handover.
   - Who becomes involved at each stage?
   - What information moves between them?

3. How do you keep track of active delivery orders?
   - How do you know what needs attention next?
   - What happens when the expected sequence changes?

## Pain points

4. What are the most frustrating parts of managing delivery orders?
   - Can you describe a recent example?
   - What effect did it have on the shift?

5. Tell me about a time when managing delivery orders went particularly well.
   - What helped it go well?
   - What would make that experience repeatable?

## Coordination with riders and customers

6. Tell me how your team coordinates with riders.
   - What happens when a rider arrives earlier or later than expected?
   - How is an order checked at handover?

7. Walk me through what happens when a delivery customer needs help.
   - How does the restaurant learn about the issue?
   - How do you decide what to do next?

## Success and priorities

8. What would a successful delivery shift look like for you?
   - What outcomes would tell you it went well?
   - Which outcome matters most?

## Wrap-up

9. What else should we understand about managing delivery orders at your restaurant?
   - Which situation should we observe directly?
   - Who else should we speak with?

If time runs short, cut Q3 first and then Q5; their topics partially overlap with Q2 and Q8. Retain Q2, Q6, Q7, and Q8 because they cover the end-to-end workflow, coordination, exceptions, and success.
